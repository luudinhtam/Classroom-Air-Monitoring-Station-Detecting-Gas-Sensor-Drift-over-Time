Dat tep sqljdbc4.jar vao dung thu muc nay.

Duong dan day du phai la:
    UserManagement/web/WEB-INF/lib/sqljdbc4.jar

Day la thu vien duy nhat cua du an. Khong them bat ky tep jar nao khac.
Dat o day thi mot ban sao dung cho ca luc bien dich lan luc chay, vi
nbproject/project.properties da tro toi dung duong dan nay.

Sau khi chep jar vao, trong NetBeans bam chuot phai vao du an, chon
Properties, muc Libraries, kiem tra thay sqljdbc4.jar trong danh sach.
Neu chua thay thi bam Add JAR/Folder roi chon dung tep vua chep.

Xoa tep ghi chu nay sau khi da dat jar vao.
